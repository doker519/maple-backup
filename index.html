features

Everything backup should be. Nothing it shouldn't.

No account. No dashboard to configure. Your bookmarks stay on your device unless you choose to back them up to your own Google Drive.

⏱

Creates backups automatically

Runs on a schedule you choose — every 1 to 7 days — in the background. Nothing to remember, nothing to click.

💾

Saved safely on your device

Every backup is stored locally first. Browse the full history, view any past snapshot, or download it as a standalone file.

↺

Restore in one click

Pick any past backup and restore it into a new "Imported" folder — your current bookmarks are never touched or overwritten.

☁

Optional Google Drive backup

Turn it on and every backup is also saved to your own Drive — off by default, disconnect anytime.

🌐

15 languages

The full interface is translated, not machine-patched — reviewed language by language.

◎

No tracking, ever

No analytics, no telemetry, no server of our own. Your bookmarks never leave your device unless you decide otherwise.

cloud backup

Your Google Drive, on your terms.

Cloud Backup is opt-in and uses Google's drive.file scope for limited Drive access.

drive.file scope

Maple Backup only accesses the backup files it creates

Maple Backup uses Google's drive.file scope, which limits access to files created or opened by the app. It cannot browse, read, or modify other files already in your Drive.

  • Off by default — starts only after you click Connect
  • Writes only to a folder this extension itself creates
  • One-way mirror — never reads or restores back out of Drive
  • Disconnect anytime; already-uploaded backups stay untouched
