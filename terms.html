The service

Maple Backup automatically creates local, point-in-time backups of your browser bookmarks, lets you view and restore past backups, and can optionally save copies of backups to a cloud storage provider of your choice — Google Drive, Microsoft OneDrive, Dropbox, Nutstore, or Koofr. It is provided free of charge, with no account, subscription, or payment of any kind.

Your data

Your bookmarks and backup history belong to you and stay on your own device unless you explicitly turn on Cloud Backup for one or more providers, in which case copies are written only to your own account with that provider — never to a server we operate.
